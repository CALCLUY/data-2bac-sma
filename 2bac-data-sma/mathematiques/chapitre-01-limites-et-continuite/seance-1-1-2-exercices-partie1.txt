#### 2-1/ Exercice 1-1

Calculer les limites suivantes :

$$\overline{\left.\right)1} \underset{x \rightarrow 4}{lim} \frac{\left|x^{2} - 2 x\right| - 8}{x^{2} - 5 x + 4} \\ \\ \overline{\left.\right)2} \underset{x \rightarrow + \infty}{lim} \frac{\sqrt{x^{2} + x + 1} - \sqrt{x^{2} - 4}}{x + \sqrt{x + 2}} \\ \\ \overline{\left.\right)3} \underset{x \rightarrow - \infty}{lim} \sqrt{x^{4} - x^{3}} - x^{2} \\ \\ \overline{\left.\right)4} \underset{x \rightarrow + \infty}{lim} x \sqrt{4 x^{2} + 3 x - 7} - 2 x^{2}$$

#### 2-2/ Exercice 1-2

Calculer les limites suivantes :

$$\overline{\left.\right)1} \underset{x \rightarrow - \infty}{lim} \frac{\sqrt{10 x^{2} + 9} - 7}{\sqrt{2 - x} + \sqrt{x^{2} + 5} - 5} \\ \\ \overline{\left.\right)2} \underset{x \rightarrow - \infty}{lim} \frac{\sqrt{x^{4} + 1} - \sqrt{x^{4} - 1}}{\sqrt{x^{2} + 1} - \sqrt{x^{2} - 1}} \\ \\ \overline{\left.\right)3} \underset{x \rightarrow + \infty}{lim} \sqrt{x^{3} + \alpha x^{2} + x + 1} \left(\alpha \in \mathbb{R}\right) \\ \\ \overline{\left.\right)4} \underset{x \rightarrow \alpha^{+}}{lim} \frac{\sqrt{x^{2} - \alpha x} + \sqrt{x^{2} - \alpha^{2}}}{\sqrt{x - \alpha}} \left(\alpha \in \mathbb{R}\right)$$

#### 2-3/ Exercice 1-3

Calculer les limites suivantes :

$$\overline{\left.\right)1} \underset{x \rightarrow \frac{\pi}{4}}{lim} \frac{x + x cos 4 x}{sin x - cos x } \\ \\ \overline{\left.\right)2} \underset{x \rightarrow \frac{\pi}{2}}{lim} \frac{2 tan 2 x - \sqrt{3} cos x}{\pi - 2 x} \\ \\ \overline{\left.\right)3} \underset{x \rightarrow \frac{\pi}{2}}{lim} \left(x tan x - \frac{\pi}{2 cos x}\right) \\ \\ \overline{\left.\right)4} \underset{x \rightarrow \frac{\pi}{3}}{lim} \frac{\sqrt{3} sin x - cos x - 1}{2 cos x + cos 3 x}$$

#### 2-4/ Exercice 1-4

Calculer les limites suivantes :

$$\overline{\left.\right)1} \underset{x \rightarrow \frac{\pi}{2}}{lim} \left(\frac{\pi}{2} - x\right) tan x \\ \\ \overline{\left.\right)2} \underset{x \rightarrow 1}{lim} \left(2 - 3 x + x^{2}\right) tan \left(\frac{\pi}{2} x\right) \\ \\ \overline{\left.\right)3} \underset{x \rightarrow + \infty}{lim} \left(x^{2} + x - 1\right) sin^{2} \left(\frac{1}{x}\right) \\ \\ \overline{\left.\right)4} \underset{x \rightarrow \frac{\pi}{4}}{lim} \frac{cos^{3} x - sin^{3} x}{sin \left(\frac{\pi}{4} - x\right)} \\ \\ \overline{\left.\right)5} \underset{x \rightarrow 0}{lim} \frac{cos a x - cos b x}{x^{2}} \left(\left(a , b\right) \in \mathbb{R}^{2}\right)$$
